# Teledyne LeCroy Interconnect Equalization Optimization Methodology

As data center networks continue to scale, maintaining signal integrity in high-speed interconnects has become an increasingly important engineering challenge. With the growing adoption of 100G, 400G, and 800G Ethernet, equalization tuning plays a critical role in ensuring reliable signal transmission and interoperability between hosts and transceivers.

For high-speed cable and interconnect manufacturers, validating signal integrity often requires extensive manual equalization tuning, which can be time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to human error.

To improve testing efficiency, accuracy, and repeatability, Teledyne LeCroy Interconnect Equalization Optimization Methodology provides an automated approach to equalization optimization. By leveraging Xena’s high-performance Z800 Freya <https://xenanetworks.com/freya-800g-pam4-ethernet-testing-112gbps-serdes/>_ Ethernet test equipment, manufacturers can efficiently optimize equalization settings and evaluate the signal integrity performance of their transceivers and cables.
