Introduction
============

As data center networks continue to scale, ensuring optimal signal integrity in high-speed interconnects has become a critical engineering challenge. With the increasing adoption of 100G, 400G, 800G transceivers, equlization tuning play a major role in making sure transceivers and hosts work together smoothly. 

For high-speed cable and interconnect manufacturers, validating signal integrity often requires extensive manual equalization tuning, which can be time-consuming, labor-intensive, and prone to human error.

To increase work efficiency and accuracy, **Teledyne LeCroy Interconnect Equalization Optimization Methodology** provides an automated cable optimization method, allowing customers to quickly and accurately test their transceivers and cables with Xena's high-performance test equipment `Z800 Freya <https://xenanetworks.com/freya-800g-pam4-ethernet-testing-112gbps-serdes/>`_.
